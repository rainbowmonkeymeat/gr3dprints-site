About GR3D Prints

Welcome to GR3D Prints! I'm just a Mom working out of my garage with a couple of Bambu Lab X1C printers. I serve the greater Grand Rapids area, and the shop is in Wayland.

I got my first printer in 2010 and love exploring the ever-evolving capabilities of these amazing machines.

Check out our shop for available prints, or check the Find Our Prints page to browse a rotating selection of prints in person.
